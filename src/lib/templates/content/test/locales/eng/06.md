# The Messiah Has Come: Hope Fulfilled

## 06.01 400 years of waiting

*The promise is still awaited — what has changed?*

The people of Israel had been waiting over 400 years
since the last prophet had spoken God's message to them.
They were waiting for the Messiah — the anointed one, the promised King — to come.
At that time, a cruel and foreign empire, the Roman Empire, had invaded and taken over Israel.
Israel's own king, eager to keep power, made friends with the Roman leaders
and ruled harshly over Israel.

## 06.02 A heavy burden

*Poverty, power — is there any hope?*

To protect his position,
he was even prepared to murder infants.
On top of that, the Romans imposed such harsh taxes
that the people of Israel fell into poverty
and hardship.
Surely this was the time for the long-awaited Messiah to come.

## 06.03 An angel appears to Mary

*For a young woman — unexpected news.*

One day, in a small town,
an angel of God appeared to Mary, a young virgin.
The angel told her that by the power of the Holy Spirit she would have a son who would be called the Son of God,
and that his name would be Jesus — “The Lord saves!”
Mary was engaged to be married to a man named Joseph.

## 06.04 An angel appears to Joseph

*Doubt — and then, reassurance.*

When Joseph heard that she was pregnant, he was ready to divorce her quietly.
But an angel of the Lord appeared to him and told him that the child had been conceived by the Holy Spirit,
that he should marry her, and that the baby should be named Jesus.

## 06.05 The road to Bethlehem

*No room to be found — yet prophecy is fulfilled.*

Because the Roman government had issued a decree that everyone had to register again in their hometown,
Joseph and Mary set out for Bethlehem.
Because so many travelers filled the region at that time,
there was no room for them anywhere.
But one man offered them a humble space.
The place where Jesus was born was a very simple stable
— this was so that the prophecy given to God's people through the prophets would be fulfilled.

## 06.06 Shepherds in the fields

*The night sky suddenly fills with light.*

In that region, on a hillside, some people were tending their sheep
when suddenly an angel of God appeared to them.
They were terrified.
The angel calmed them and told them good news that would bring joy to all people.

## 06.07 “Good news of great joy”

*What does the heavenly host sing?*

They said, “Today, in the city of David, the Messiah is born,”
and told them they would find him lying in a manger.
Suddenly a vast heavenly host appeared in the sky, singing praise.
When the angels had gone, the shepherds hurried to see the newborn.
They found the child exactly where they had been told, and after worshiping him, they went out and told others what they had seen.

## 06.08 A prophecy spoken at the temple

*How long had one old man been waiting?*

When Joseph and Mary brought the baby to the temple to be presented and blessed,
there was an old man there —
a prophet who had prayed to see the Messiah before he died,
and had waited many years.
He spoke a prophecy: “This child, whom they have long awaited,
will be a light not only to Israel but to all nations,
and will bring great sorrow to his parents.”

## 06.09 The prophetess Anna

*Another witness, another recognition.*

After that, a prophetess who had been calling on God's name
and fasting for many years also came
and prophesied that Jesus would be the Savior of Israel.

## 06.10 Wise men from the East

*Where does the star lead them?*

Not long after, wise men from the east came to the local king,
saying they had followed a bright star in the sky
that revealed the birth of a new king,
and that they had come looking for that king.
The local king then asked his religious leaders where, according to the prophecies,
the Messiah — the long-awaited King — was to be born.
His religious leaders answered him, “The King must be born in Bethlehem.”

## 06.11 Sent on to Bethlehem

*The king asks — but for a different reason.*

Hearing this news, the king was deeply troubled.
He sent for the wise men from the east and asked them, if they found that King, to bring word back to him too —
and sent them on their way.
Guided by the star in the sky, the wise men came to the house where Jesus was.

## 06.12 Gifts and a warning

*A message through a dream changes everything.*

After entering the house,
they found the child lying in a manger.
They offered him gifts of gold,
frankincense, and myrrh.
After this, the wise men did not report back to the local king where they had found the newborn child,
because they had been warned in a dream during the night not to go back and tell him.

## 06.13 Flight to Egypt

*One order — and a terrible consequence.*

So Joseph took Mary and their son and fled to the land of Egypt.
The local king became furious
and gave orders to find and kill every child under two years old in that region,
because he feared that this newborn king would take away his kingdom and power.

## 06.14 Return to Nazareth

*The danger isn't over — but they know where to settle.*

Some time later, after the local king died,
Joseph and Mary returned from Egypt with their son Jesus.
But having been warned in a dream that the next king was very dangerous,
they went instead to a small town
in the northern part of Nazareth.

## 06.15 Prophecy fulfilled, point by point

*Foretold 400 years earlier — now unfolding before our eyes.*

In this brief account,
we see that Jesus' life matched the prophecies spoken 400 years earlier.
They had foretold that the Messiah would be born in Bethlehem to a pure young woman,
that the local people would be filled with grief over the deaths of their children,
that the Messiah would be taken to Egypt,
and that he would be called a Nazarene.
They had also foretold that this King would be a descendant of David.

## 06.16 The meaning of the gifts

*Gold, frankincense, myrrh — what do they mean?*

The gifts brought by the wise men
— who likely came from Persia or Central Asia — were also deeply significant.
Gold was traditionally offered to kings.
Frankincense was offered to priests.
And myrrh was used to wrap a body for burial.

## 06.17 Light enters the darkness

*This is the turning point of history.*

Jesus came into this world to establish a new kingdom,
to be a priest for all people for all time, and to be sacrificed for everyone.
In Jesus, God broke into the darkness of this world out of his love for humanity,
to fulfill the prophecy spoken in the Old Testament and to save this world.
And so we arrive at the pinnacle of humanity's story:
the renewal of our relationship with God, with one another, and with the world around us.
