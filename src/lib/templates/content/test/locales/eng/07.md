# Life of Jesus (Part 1)

## 07.01 The wedding at Cana

*The wine runs out — but what does Jesus do?*

Jesus grew up in a rural village.
When he was a young man, before he had started his ministry, he went to a wedding with his family.
In the middle of this wedding, his mother came to tell Jesus that they had run out of wine.
This would bring huge shame on the family of the bride. Jesus replied that his time had not yet come.
However, she told the servants to do exactly what Jesus said.
There were large vessels at the door for people to wash their feet when they entered.
Jesus told the servants to fill these with water and then to serve the contents of these vessels.
When they brought it out, it was wine, and the master of ceremonies drank it and said to the father of the bride that he had kept the best wine for last.
The family was honored. This is a picture of what Jesus was about to do.

## 07.02 A picture of what's to come

*This isn't just a story about a miracle.*

He was going to give his life to bring cleansing for all people, and take away all our shame.
He himself was going to bring a new road to a holy life —
not through external rules, not through some miraculous transformation for its own sake —
but a new, joyful life that would bring us into a loving relationship with God.

## 07.03 The voice of John

*A voice in the wilderness — calling to what?*

Later there was a man called John, who went out into the countryside by the River Jordan,
where he called out to the people to come and turn back to God,
because the Kingdom of Heaven was near.
People came out to him, confessed their wrongdoings, and were baptized by John.

## 07.04 “One greater is coming”

*John makes clear exactly who he is.*

However, John told them that there was someone greater who was coming,
whose shoelace he was not worthy to untie,
and who would baptize them with the Holy Spirit.
One day John saw Jesus coming and told them that this was the man about whom he had been telling them.

## 07.05 Jesus is baptized

*The heavens open — what is heard?*

Jesus came to the river and asked to be baptized.
John tried to stop him and said, “I need to be baptized by you!”
But Jesus said that for now this had to be done.
As Jesus came up out of the water, the heavens opened, and John saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove and alighting on Jesus,
and a voice from heaven said, “This is my Son, with whom I am well pleased.”
Now, from this point, we see Jesus beginning his ministry by coming down to the river to be baptized.

## 07.06 Why was he baptized?

*If he was sinless, then why?*

Why?
Jesus, who had done nothing wrong, who was without sin,
walked into the Jordan River to take on the responsibility for all our wrongdoing,
so that he could lead us on.
Others were going into the river because of their own wrongdoing.
Everything is about to change.

## 07.07 Fully God, fully man

*He steps into our place.*

Instead of us struggling to get to God,
God comes to us to lead us out of our slavery to human sin and into freedom.
Here we see the Son of God going down into the water,
and the Holy Spirit descending on him like a dove,
and the Lord speaking to declare that this is his Son.
This is the one everyone had been waiting for — the Messiah —
and right at the start of his ministry,
he steps into our place,
fully human,
and here revealed as being one with God the Father and the Holy Spirit.

## 07.08 Temptation in the wilderness

*What does Satan offer?*

Immediately after his baptism, Jesus was led by Satan into the wilderness,
where Jesus fasted and prayed for a long time.
Here Satan tempted Jesus to use his powers for personal gain and greed.
He tempted Jesus to make a demonstration of power rather than do the work he came to do;
he tempted Jesus to do evil for personal political power.
Jesus in this way entered into the total human experience of temptation to turn away from God's ways.

## 07.09 He resisted

*Every time — with one answer.*

But unlike us, he resisted —
each time using the words of Scripture.
He resisted every tempting way out of his difficult and painful mission.
Why?
Because he loved us, and he had come to redeem us.

## 07.10 At the synagogue

*Ancient words — a new meaning.*

After resisting evil, Satan left,
and Jesus went to preach at the local place of Jewish worship.
He was asked to read from the old scriptures, and he read aloud the prophecies about himself:

## 07.11 “The Spirit of the Lord is upon me”

*A prophecy — about himself.*

“The Spirit of the Lord is on me, because he has anointed me to proclaim good news to the poor.
He has sent me to proclaim freedom for the prisoners and recovery of sight for the blind,
to set the oppressed free, to proclaim the year of the Lord's favour.”
It was clear to his listeners that he was announcing he had come to fulfill the prophecies about the Messiah,
and here he shows how the Holy Spirit's work in him would be revealed,
both in body and in spirit.

## 07.12 Ministry in Galilee

*Teaching and healing go hand in hand.*

Jesus then went throughout Galilee, beside a large lake.
He went out teaching, preaching, and healing people.
News about him spread; crowds came, and people with every kind of disease were healed.
As he went out to teach people about God, he used many stories.

## 07.13 The lost sheep

*99 stay behind — but He searches for the one.*

For example, Jesus told them about a man who had a hundred sheep.
He left the 99 at home and went out to search for the one that was lost.
When he found it, he put the sheep on his shoulders and carried it home rejoicing,
telling his neighbors he had found his lost sheep!
In this story, we can picture ourselves as the sheep.
God's love is so strong that he searches for us when we are lost, to bring us back to himself.
He wants all people to be with him — not just a select few.

## 07.14 “I am the good shepherd”

*The sheep know Him — and He knows them.*

Jesus said, “I am the good shepherd;
I know my sheep, and my sheep know me — just as the Father knows me and I know the Father.
I lay down my life for the sheep.
I have other sheep that are not of this sheep pen.
I must bring them also.
I lay down my life, only to take it up again.
No one takes it from me, but I lay it down of my own accord.
I have authority to lay it down, and authority to take it up again.
This command I received from my Father.”

## 07.15 “I AM WHO I AM”

*Words once spoken to Moses — now from His own mouth.*

When Jesus said “I am the good shepherd,” this echoed the words God spoke to Moses in the Old Testament,
when Moses asked what he should say if the king of Egypt asked who had sent him —
and God answered, “I AM WHO I AM.”
So when Jesus says he is the good shepherd,
laying down his life for his sheep and taking it up again,
he is telling them that his authority to die and rise again comes from God.

## 07.16 Calling God “Father”

*This is more than just a word.*

Jesus calls God his “Father,” and later invites us to call him “Father” too.
This does not mean God is like a human father in the flesh.
Rather, God is the one true Father of us all. Human fathers are meant to be like him.

## 07.17 Reaching out in faith

*Years of illness — is one touch enough?*

As Jesus was walking by the water with a crowd following him,
the leader of the synagogue came and asked him to come to his home, because his daughter was dying.
On his way to the house, a woman who had been bleeding for years touched Jesus' clothes,
hoping to be healed.
As soon as she touched his clothes, she was instantly healed.
Jesus told her it was by her faith that she had been healed.

## 07.18 When told, “Your daughter is dead”

*Is it too late?*

So, as they were getting close to the house, people came and told him that his daughter had died.
But when Jesus heard this, he told the leader of the synagogue not to be afraid, only believe,
and went into the house
and raised the girl back to life.
This bleeding woman was considered unclean at that time.
By speaking with her in front of everyone, Jesus turned the woman's shame into honor.
The leader of the synagogue risked being condemned for inviting Jesus into his home after Jesus had touched an unclean person —
but he trusted Jesus, and Jesus healed his daughter.

## 07.19 Something entirely new

*Uncleanness now flows in the other direction.*

Jesus was doing something completely new!
An ordinary man would become unclean by touching a sinful or sick person.
But with Jesus, this was completely reversed.
When he touches the unclean, he makes the unclean clean — not the other way around.
He cleansed the woman and healed the girl.
This is no ordinary man!
