# The Beginning

## 01.01 Opening questions

*Who is God, really — and what does He have to do with you?*

What is Christianity?
Who is God?
What does he have to do with me?
Does he love me?
What does God want
me to do with my life?
Does he care about my life?
We may have many questions,
or we may just want
to know a bit
about what others believe.

## 01.02 Series purpose

*Want to know the truth about God?*

These videos aim
to give an overview of God,
his care,
and his plan
for people's lives.

## 01.03 Before creation

*What existed before the universe began?*

In the very beginning,
before the creation of the universe,
God was.
The earth
was formless,
and God's Spirit
hovered over the waters.

## 01.04 Let there be light

*The first word was spoken — and light appeared.*

By his Word alone,
God created this world
and every living thing in it.
He said,
“Let there be light,”
and light appeared,
and God saw that it was good.

## 01.05 Sky, sea, land, and plants

*Ocean, sky, land, plants — all spoken into being.*

By that same Word, he also made the ocean and the sky.
He created the land
and was pleased that it was good.
Then God made plants and trees —
fruit trees of every kind, in great abundance.

## 01.06 Sea creatures, birds, and land animals

*Fish, birds, animals — the world fills with life.*

It was plentiful. Then God said,
“Let the waters teem with fish and other life,
and let the skies be filled
with birds of every kind.”
Seeing all this, God was pleased,
and he blessed them
and told them to multiply.
God made the wild animals
and the livestock,
and he made mighty rivers of clear water.

## 01.07 The garden and humanity, made in God's image

*A garden appears... and God creates humanity in His own image.*

Here he made a plentiful garden,
and in that same garden God created man and woman.
He made them in his own image, saying,
“Let us make human beings in our image, in our likeness.”
Reflecting God's own nature, we were given responsibility over everything living on the earth —

## 01.08 Humanity's responsibility

*Given responsibility over everything — see what that really means.*

— the fish in the sea, the birds in the sky,
the livestock, and every kind of animal on the earth.
When creation was finally complete, the Creator rested
and set apart a day of rest for everyone — its whole purpose was simply to be with God.

## 01.09 The day of rest

*Why did God rest? The answer might surprise you.*

This day of rest gives every living thing a chance to breathe,
and it's essential for sustaining the vitality of creation.
God was pleased with the world he had made, and he created us to enjoy his presence here —

## 01.10 Purpose: delighting in God's presence

*You weren't made just to exist — see the original plan.*

— to enjoy his creation,
and to be entrusted with caring for it, expanding his garden, and bringing his presence into the world.
In calling it good, he gave value to it all.

## 01.11 Relationship, marriage, and purity

*The first marriage — no shame, full intimacy.*

Man and woman were made to be in relationship — with God, with each other, and with the world he created, living in his presence.
He wanted joy for his people.
God said a man would leave his father and mother to become one with his wife.
They found joy in each other's company.
They were meant to look outward, beyond themselves, toward one another's well-being.
They were naked, yet felt no shame!

## 01.12 Two trees and a warning

*Two trees, one warning — the choice that changed everything.*

In the middle of that garden stood the Tree of Life, and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.
God warned them that if they ate from the tree of good and evil, they would die.

## 01.13 Abundance, trust, and a warning

*They had it all — but it all rested on one condition.*

In other words, humanity had it all.
We had a beautiful creation, water flowing from the rivers, trees heavy with fruit.
We had relationship with God,
with one another, and with the land.
We had life, and God — who had been faithful to us —
lovingly warned us that life would come through being faithful to him:
through following his wishes and commands, and accepting his way instead of our own.
If we went our own way, we would lose that life.

## 01.14 The temptation

*A serpent, a twisted word, and a bite that changed history.*

So how did we handle the task we were given? What happened next?
We didn't manage it well at all. We were tempted.
A serpent twisted God's words to deceive the woman,
and even though they knew what God had said, they ate the fruit of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.
In other words, they chose to go their own way instead of God's.

## 01.15 Realization and hiding

*Shame struck suddenly — and they hid from the God who loved them.*

In that instant, they both “saw what was really going on” — they saw that they were naked!
They sewed fig leaves together as makeshift clothing, and when they heard God walking in the garden,
the man and his wife hid among the trees — they hid from God.

## 01.16 Confronted and blamed

*Caught. And now everyone's pointing fingers.*

God, seeing their shame,
asked whether they had eaten from the forbidden tree.
The woman tried to blame the serpent,
and the man tried
to blame the woman —
but God did not accept it.

## 01.17 The narrator's turn

*This isn't just their story. It's yours too.*

This is our story.
It isn't really about how the earth was formed —
it's about who God is, and who we are.
God loves us; God created us for a lifetime of unity and relationship with him.

## 01.18 Loved, and betrayed

*Fully loved — and still betrayed. Sound familiar?*

God gave us everything we could need or want.
But we walked away from his ways, betraying the very God who loves and cares for us.

## 01.19 The great reversal

*Freedom to shame. Peace to fear. See how fast it all turned.*

We used to walk in freedom — now we're left with shame.
We used to have peace — now we live under guilt.
Where there used to be ease, fear has taken its place.

## 01.20 Death instead of life

*Cut off from the source of life — see what was lost.*

Humanity went looking for life apart from the one who had given them breath.
Instead of gain, death came to us —
the death of our bodies, and the loss of purpose.
Worst of all, we cut ourselves off from the very author of life.

## 01.21 Driven from the garden

*Paradise was lost — and the door closed behind them.*

As a result of their choice, the world changed —
they were forced to leave that wonderful garden.
humanity's own personal relationship with God, and their trust in him, was broken.

## 01.22 Human relationships break down

*Envy, hatred — the first wound between people.*

People lost relationship with one another.
Man and woman's relationship became unequal,
envy and hatred entered their hearts,
and they began killing one another.

## 01.23 Hardship enters creation

*Suffering entered the world — and it's still here.*

Hardship and sickness entered the world.
As the land grew harsher, human labor grew harder.
We don't need to look far to see the suffering and hardship that comes from what humanity has done —
because we truly did lose our relationship with God.

## 01.24 God does not abandon them

*Even now, God doesn't walk away. See why.*

But God did not leave them.
He kept loving them and speaking with them.
He warned them that they would be accountable for what they had done —
yet his loving purpose for us never changed.

## 01.25 The promise hidden in the curse

*Hidden inside the curse — the first promise of rescue.*

God foretells what's coming: speaking to the serpent, he says that it will not only be cursed,
but that it will strike the heel of Eve's descendant — and that descendant will crush its head.
In this, God points ahead to how he will save the world.

## 01.26 A glimpse of the rescue to come

*A death that will change everything draws near. The first glimpse.*

We're about to see how God will bring the very best to his beloved children through the worst kind of death —
how he will crush the head of evil, save us, and carry out his purpose.

## 01.27 Series roadmap / closing

*This is just the beginning. Where does the story go from here?*

As we go through this story, we'll dig deeper into who God is.
We'll learn why he made us, and what his purpose for us here is.
We'll see how he rescues us from the curse of evil,
and how he brings us into full fellowship with him.
