# The Holy Spirit: Living in God's Love

## 10.01 Pentecost begins

*Something like tongues of fire — what does it mean?*

While waiting in Jerusalem
they gathered to pray.
One day suddenly they saw
what seemed to be tongues of fire
that separated
and came to rest on each of them.

## 10.02 Speaking in other languages

*People from many nations — why are they amazed?*

All of them were filled with the Holy Spirit
and began to speak in other languages as the Spirit enabled them.
People came from many countries with different languages, and they were amazed
because they could all hear them speaking in their own language.

## 10.03 Peter's sermon

*Some mock — but what does Peter say?*

Some mocked and said they were drunk.
However, Peter stood up and spoke to the crowds,
explaining who Jesus — the man whom they had crucified —
really was,
and told them that this was just as the prophets had foretold.

## 10.04 3,000 believe

*What changed in a single day?*

He called them to come and be baptized,
and that day 3,000 people turned to Christ,
and every day more people started turning to follow Jesus.

## 10.05 Everything in common

*What did the believers' life look like?*

Their lives were completely changed.
All the believers were together and shared everything in common.
They sold property and possessions to give to anyone who had need.
They were in awe of the miracles that God did through his apostles.

## 10.06 From fear to wonder

*Where did the apostles' miracles lead?*

Jesus' followers were transformed from fearful, uneducated young men
into courageous, caring men who taught and told people about Jesus and who he was.

## 10.07 From fisherman to leader

*Who did Peter become?*

Peter, who had been a fisherman, had walked with Jesus
and recognized that Jesus was the promised Messiah.
However, he had also denied that he knew Jesus, out of fear, when Jesus was going for trial.
Peter had gathered with others in a room, confused and fearful, after Jesus was killed,
and did not immediately understand what had happened when the women told him that Jesus was risen.

## 10.08 No longer afraid

*He once denied Him — now what does he do?*

Now he had met the risen Christ, had been filled with the Holy Spirit,
and was transformed.
No longer fearful, he went out to the public square preaching about Jesus,
courageously refusing to be silenced by religious leaders who had told him not to preach,

## 10.09 A paralyzed man is healed

*Miracles from ordinary men — what do the leaders say?*

and he healed people and performed miracles in Jesus' name.
The leaders who wanted to oppose them did not know what to do,
as the crowds were listening.
When they saw the courage of Peter and John
and realized that they were unschooled, ordinary men,
they were astonished,
and they took note that these men had been with Jesus.
But since they could see a man who had been healed standing there with them,
there was nothing they could say, so they let them go.

## 10.10 The movement spreads

*Growing right alongside the opposition.*

The number of followers of Jesus increased each day
— although so did the opposition.
People were becoming followers of Jesus in Israel, Syria, Turkey, Europe, Africa,
and across Central, East, and South Asia.

## 10.11 Persecution and truth

*Who suffers for a lie?*

Most of the close followers, who had walked with Jesus and seen him die and rise again,
and who were filled with the Holy Spirit,
went on to be imprisoned, tortured, and killed in dreadful ways.
If they had made up the story,
why would they not just admit it and avoid all that misery?
If it is true that Jesus died and rose again and sent the Holy Spirit upon us,
then yes — it is good news worth living and dying for!

## 10.12 Saul the persecutor

*Who was throwing Christians in prison?*

A well-educated religious leader called Paul
— highly regarded as following the law of God —
began to go and have Christians put in jail.
On his way to a town called Damascus,
where he was going to seek out Christians to arrest them,
he had a dramatic spiritual encounter with Jesus that completely amazed him.

## 10.13 On the road to Damascus

*One light — and everything changes.*

He immediately became blind and was led into Damascus.
God spoke to a man called Ananias
and told him to go and pray for Paul's healing.
God also revealed to Paul that he would come and heal him.
When Ananias prayed,
Paul's eyesight returned,

## 10.14 Paul begins to preach

*From persecutor to preacher.*

and Paul began to go out and teach who Jesus is,
and went all over present-day Turkey, Italy, and Spain
to tell people the good news that Jesus was indeed God incarnate
who had come to give us new life in him,
in relationship with God and the Holy Spirit.
God called him to take the good news of Jesus to all nations.

## 10.15 Paul's letters

*Who is God, and what lies ahead?*

He wrote letters at the time which give us a greater understanding of who Jesus was, what he had done,
and that our God is one God in three persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit —
a God of relationship.
In the future, Jesus will come back, and at that time all people will recognize him
and will bow down at his feet, and all will be made new.
The whole creation is waiting for that day,
when all things and all people will be reconciled to God and transformed, and all God's creation renewed.

## 10.16 An invitation

*It's offered to you too.*

We invite you now to take up the invitation to talk more,
and take up this opportunity to receive the Lord's gift of love and his Holy Spirit.
